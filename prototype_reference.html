FAQ

How do I book?

Choose services on the Book page, pick a date and time, enter your details, and confirm. Your request goes to us on WhatsApp.

Do you offer home service?

Yes. Select “At Home” while booking and add your address. A small visit fee applies.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Please message us on WhatsApp at least 24 hours before your slot.

Do you do bridal packages?

Yes – add Bridal Makeup and mention your date in the notes, and we will confirm a trial.
